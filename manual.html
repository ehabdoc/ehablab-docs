00 Before you start: what this app is, and is not

Three minutes here saves a wrong expectation later.

✓ What it does
  • 2,402 questions across eight departments, each with four options and an explanation that says why the key is right and why every distractor is wrong.
  • Builds a mock exam weighted like the ASCP outline and scores it instantly against a 65% pass mark.
  • Schedules your review with SM-2 (1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days) so what you studied does not fade.
  • Runs entirely offline: the bank and its images ship inside the app.
✕ What it does not do
  • It is not a medical reference: no diagnosis, no clinical decision — it is an exam study tool.
  • It does not translate medical text into Arabic; the explanation stays precise English.
  • It does not download images: images come from your own source only.
  • It does not claim every question is board-level — part of the bank is direct concept drilling.
Why the medical text is English

The chrome — buttons, headings, settings — is Arabic or English as you choose, but the stem, the options and the explanation stay English and LTR, because the exam itself is English and translating a lab term costs precision. That is a deliberate decision, not missing translation.

  1. افتح التطبيق وسجّل الدخول ببريد إلكتروني. الدخول يعمل أيضاً دون إنترنت — البريد يُحفظ على الجهاز.

  5. افتح «Mock exam» عندما تريد اختباراً موقوتاً بأوزان ASCP، ثم راجع Analytics لترى توقع النجاح.

01 Quick start in a minute

If you want the result now and not the whole guide, take these five steps.

  1. Open the app and sign in with an email. Sign-in also works offline — the email is stored on the device.
  2. Tap Continue on Home to resume the department you studied last, or pick one from the grid.
  3. Tap an option — the explanation appears at once: why the key is right, and why every other option is wrong.
  4. Grade your recall with Again / Hard / Good / Easy. That grade sets the next review date.
  5. Open Mock exam when you want a timed, ASCP-weighted test, then check Analytics for your pass prediction.
Quick save

A question you want to keep: tap the bookmark icon in the question bar and it lands in Library → Bookmarks. Anything you get wrong is added to Mistakes automatically.

02 Install & first sign-in

First launch is four short steps; after that it never needs a connection.

  1. Install the app — an APK on Android, or open the web build in any modern browser.
  2. Wait one second on first launch: the app reads the bundled bank (2,402 questions, about 4.8 MB) and prepares the local cache.
  3. Sign in with email and password when the cloud keys are configured, or with email alone in the offline build.
  4. Confirm your email with the six-digit code that arrives in your inbox — once.
If the code never arrives

The code comes from the app's own mail sender, not the default Supabase one. Check spam, then tap Resend code. If it keeps failing, the cause is the SMTP configuration, not your device.

03 Home

Everything starts here: continue, test yourself, or clear what is due.

ElementWhat it does

Why does it show 0% — or 40% after one right answer?

Mastery is not "percent solved", it is a rolling average. For the first five answers each response carries a 0.4 weight, so a single correct answer reads 40% and climbs as answers accumulate. The number settles after about ten answers.

04 Study mode

The quiet mode: no clock, and an explanation the moment you answer.

What each tap does

  • Tapping an option locks the answer and reveals the explanation immediately — there is no separate "check" button.
  • The correct option turns green, a wrong pick turns red, and the key is always shown even if you never chose it.
  • The moment of selection records a provisional medium grade; the grade buttons refine it. A fast answer does not mean the question was logged as "easy".
  • In exam mode no explanation appears, and nothing is recorded until you submit.

The bottom bar

ButtonWhat it does
Prev / NextMove between questions without losing answers.
StrikeCrosses out an option in red to eliminate it — a strike is never an answer.

The image frame only appears when there is an image

Text-only questions no longer show an empty image box. The 73 images appear only on the questions that actually own one.

٥ الشرح والتقييم

هذا هو قلب التطبيق: لماذا صحّت الإجابة، ولماذا فشل كل خيار آخر.

أربع طبقات لكل شرح

  1. الإجابة الصحيحة مع سبب مختصر مبني على المبدأ لا على الحفظ.
  2. سبب خطأ كل خيار — وهذا ما يفرّق بين «أعرف الإجابة» و«أعرف لماذا». كل خيار من الثلاثة يُشرح منفصلاً.
  3. المرجع: كتاب وإصدار وفصل حيث توفر، أو «From LabCE …» أو «Based on ASCP BOC».
  4. تلميح المدى عند وجوده: المدى الطبيعي للتحليل داخل نفس السؤال.

ماذا يفعل زر التقييم؟

لا يهبط المعامل تحت 1.3 مهما أخطأت، ولا يتجاوز 3.0 مهما تسارعت — حتى لا يخرج الجدول عن منطق المراجعة.

05 Explanation & grading

This is the heart of the app: why the key is right, and why each distractor fails.

Four layers in every explanation

  1. The correct answer with a short reason built on the principle, not on recall.
  2. Why each wrong option fails — the part that separates "I know the answer" from "I know why". Each of the three distractors is addressed.
  3. The reference: book, edition and chapter where known, or a "From LabCE …" / "Based on ASCP BOC" citation.
  4. A range hint where one exists: the analyte's reference range inside the same question.

What the grade buttons do

ButtonNext intervalEase factor
AgainOne day−0.2
HardSame rung, no advance−0.05
GoodOne rung up the 1→3→7→14→30 ladderunchanged
EasyInterval × ease factor (capped at 30 days)+0.1
A second attempt scores half

Re-answering a question you previously missed earns 0.5 instead of 1. That is deliberate: repeating a question until it sticks should not equal first-time mastery.

Ease is clamped between 1.3 and 3.0

It never falls below 1.3 however often you miss, and never exceeds 3.0 however fast you go — so the schedule keeps behaving like a review schedule.

06 Mock exam

A weighted exam like the real thing, or one department only — your call.

Two modes, not one

  • Per-department: one department only, at a count you choose — useful before a departmental exam.

If a department cannot fill its quota, the spare slots are redistributed to departments that still have questions available — questions are never dropped and the exam is never shortened.

زر «امتحان شامل جديد» يبدأ فوراً

لا يعيد فتح إعداد الامتحان؛ يستخدم الإعدادات الأخيرة. إن أردت تغيير العدد أو المؤقت، ابدأ من الرئيسية.

08 Result screen

  • Per-department is computed from this session only — it is not mixed with your history.
  • Due tomorrow shows what will pile up if you skip today.

"New mock exam" starts immediately

10 Search

Search everything, then study what you found as a session.

Four scopes: Everything, Stem, Explanation, Tags. The scope cuts noise: searching "glucose" in explanations gives a different set from searching it in stems.

No results?

The bank is written in academic English, so search the root: hemoly rather than hemolysis, anem rather than anemia. Try the explanation scope too before concluding a question is missing.

كيف يُحسب توقّع النجاح؟

12 Analytics

Score over time, mastery by department, and a pass prediction.

How pass prediction is computed

It is not the average of all your attempts. It is a weighted average of the last three attempts per department, with the most recent weighing most — where you are today matters more than where you were a month ago. So one bad mock can drop it, which is the point.

MetricWhere it comes from
Score over timeEach recorded exam score, in order
Pass predictionWeighted average of the last 3 attempts
Dept masteryFrom the SM-2 cards of that department
Avg sec / QSession time ÷ questions
No attempts, no analytics

The screen reads "No attempts yet — run a mock" until you submit your first exam. Study sessions alone do not appear here; this log is for submitted, timed exams.

13 Lab tools

Four calculators inside the app — no paper, no external calculator.

ToolWhat it computes
RangesReference ranges for hematology, chemistry and fluids.
SI converterConverts 14 analytes between conventional and SI units.
DilutionC1V1 = C2V2 for any dilution.
PT/INRINR = (PT ÷ MNPT)ISI with a therapeutic-range readout.

The sheet opens from two places: Home, and the Calc button in the question bar — without losing your answer or your place.

Why PT/INR belongs here

Because half of the coagulation questions end in an INR calculation. Having the tool on the same screen means you compute and verify without leaving the question — less time lost, and the case context stays intact.

These numbers are for practice, not for reporting

Reference ranges differ between labs and platforms. Never use these figures in a real lab report; always use your own laboratory's validated range.

١٥ الحساب والمزامنة

15 Account & sync

An account is optional for studying, and useful so you never lose progress.

What syncs

  • Content: new bank revisions are pulled when available.
  • Review cards: pushed and pulled, so the schedule is the same on every device.
  • Streak: mirrored to your account so a device change does not reset it.

Sync runs at sign-in and on every visit to Home — in practice after each graded answer, without waiting for a sign-out.

SituationWhat happens
No connectionEverything works; requests are skipped quietly and retried later.
Email unconfirmedSign-in stops at the code screen, with a resend button.
Forgot passwordA six-digit code to your inbox, then a new password — without leaving the app.
Two accounts, one deviceSigning out clears the session, not your local progress.
"Best effort" is not marketing language

If a sync fails for a network reason, the app neither blocks studying nor wipes your data. The request is retried on the next Home visit.

If the cloud project is paused

Free-tier projects pause after inactivity. The service then rejects requests and the admin shows a clear error. Local studying keeps working in full.

صيغة الاستيراد

سطر واحد لكل سؤال، بأي فاصل من هذه: | أو Tab أو ,. الترتيب:

stem|A|B|C|D|correct|explanation|dept|subtopic|difficulty|image|reference

16 Admin CMS

An admin screen, not a student one — opened with a service key entered on the spot and never stored.

TabWhat you can do
DashboardTotals, published vs draft, and per-department counts.
QuestionsSearch and filter, publish or unpublish, and a full question editor.
ImportPaste rows, preview errors line by line, then insert as drafts.
UsersRecent accounts and counts.

Import format

One row per question, with any of these separators: |, Tab or ,. Order:

stem|A|B|C|D|correct|explanation|dept|subtopic|difficulty|image|reference

The header row is optional. Every row is validated before insertion and rejected with a reason (line number + cause) if incomplete; duplicates are detected automatically.

A service key is not a login

The service_role key bypasses every access policy. Do not store it in a file, do not share it, and do not use it on a shared machine. The app keeps it in memory for that session only.

Why a service key at all?

Because the questions table is writable only by the service role — there is deliberately no write policy for the user role, so nobody edits the bank from inside the app.

١٨ أسئلة شائعة ومشاكل

أكثر ما يُسأل، بإجابة مباشرة.

هل يعمل التطبيق فعلاً بدون إنترنت؟

نعم. البنك (4.8 ميغابايت) والصور كلها داخل التطبيق. الإنترنت مطلوب فقط للمزامنة وتأكيد البريد واستعادة كلمة السر.

حليت سؤالاً صحيحاً وظهرت النسبة 40% — لماذا؟

الإتقان متوسط متحرك، وأول خمس إجابات وزنها 0.4. إجابة صحيحة واحدة = 40%، وتتضح النسبة بعد عشر إجابات. الرقم ليس «نسبة ما حللته».

لماذا الشرح بالإنجليزية ولا يوجد ترجمة؟

لأن الامتحان إنجليزي، ولأن ترجمة المصطلح المخبري تفقد دقة حاسمة (فرق MCV و RDW مثلاً). الواجهة وحدها مترجمة.

ما معنى «محاولة ثانية = نصف درجة»؟

إن أعدت سؤالاً سبق أن أخطأت فيه، الإجابة الصحيحة تُحسب 0.5. الغرض ألا يساوي التكرار حتى الحفظ إتقاناً من أول محاولة.

لم يصل رمز التأكيد إلى بريدي.

تحقّق من مجلد الرسائل غير المرغوبة، ثم اضغط «Resend code». الرمز يصل من مرسل التطبيق؛ إن تكرّر الفشل فالمشكلة في إعداد البريد على الخادم لا في جهازك.

هل تُرفع ملاحظاتي إلى السحابة؟

لا. الملاحظات محلية بالكامل، ولا تُزامَن. بطاقات المراجعة والسلسلة هي ما يُزامَن.

هل يقرأ التطبيق نتائج تحاليلي أو يتصل بجهاز؟

لا. لا يوجد أي اتصال بأجهزة المختبر ولا بملفات LIS. كل الأرقام تكتبها أنت أو تأتي مع السؤال.

أعدت تثبيت التطبيق — هل فقدت تقدّمي؟

إن كنت سجّلت الدخول وزامنت، تقدّمك في حسابك ويُستعاد. إن كنت تعمل محلياً فقط، فالتقدّم يُمسح مع بيانات التطبيق.

هل يعمل على iOS أو الحاسب؟

نسخة الويب تعمل على الحاسب وأي متصفح حديث. حزمة iOS لم تُبنَ ولم تُختبر بعد، فلا تعتمد عليها.

الإشعار اليومي لا يظهر.

يحتاج إذن الإشعارات، وقد تمنعه بعض إصدارات أندرويد من إعدادات النظام. الإشعار محلي من الجهاز، لا يعتمد على السحابة.

18 FAQ & troubleshooting

The questions that come up most, answered straight.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. The bank (4.8 MB) and all images live inside the app. A connection is needed only for sync, email confirmation and password reset.

I answered one correctly and mastery showed 40% — why?

Mastery is a rolling average where the first five answers carry a 0.4 weight. One correct answer = 40%, and the figure settles after about ten answers. It is not "percent solved".

Why is the explanation in English with no translation?

Because the exam is English, and translating a lab term loses decisive precision (MCV versus RDW, for example). Only the chrome is translated.

What does "second attempt = half credit" mean?

Re-answering a question you previously missed earns 0.5. The point is that repeating until memorised should not equal first-time mastery.

The confirmation code never arrives.

Check spam, then tap "Resend code". The code is sent by the app's own sender; if it keeps failing, the cause is the server-side mail configuration, not your device.

Are my notes uploaded to the cloud?

No. Notes are fully local and never synced. Review cards and the streak are what sync.

Does the app read my lab results or connect to an instrument?

No. There is no connection to any analyser or LIS. Every number is either typed by you or comes with the question.

I reinstalled — did I lose my progress?

If you signed in and synced, progress is on your account and comes back. If you worked purely offline, progress is cleared with the app data.

Does it run on iOS or desktop?

The web build runs on desktop and any modern browser. The iOS package has not been built or tested, so do not rely on it.

The daily reminder does not appear.

It needs notification permission, and some Android builds block it from system settings. The reminder is generated locally, not from the cloud.

19 Limits & medical notice

What the app does not do, stated plainly before you find out in an exam.

Medical notice

This app is a study tool, not a medical device and not a clinical reference. Do not use it to diagnose a patient, to make a treatment decision, or to verify a real laboratory result. Reference ranges differ between laboratories, and questions may contain deliberate simplification for teaching.
